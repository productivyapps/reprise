Delete Your Reprise Account & Data

Reprise — workout & progression tracker.

This page explains how to request deletion of your Reprise account and the personal data associated with it. You can request deletion at any time, free of charge.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to productivyapps@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Reprise account”.
  3. Send it from (or include) the email address you use to sign in to Reprise, so we can verify the request.

We confirm and complete verified deletion requests within 30 days and email you when it is done.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove all of the following:

DataAction
Account & login (email, authentication identity)Deleted
Profile and app settingsDeleted
Routines and routine exercisesDeleted
Workout history, exercises and logged setsDeleted
Custom exercises you createdDeleted

What data is kept, and for how long

  • Subscription & purchase records: If you bought a Reprise Pro subscription, the related purchase records are held by Google Play and our payments provider (RevenueCat) for as long as required by tax, accounting, and legal obligations. These records are not used to identify you after deletion and are retained only for the period required by law (typically up to 7 years). To manage or cancel the subscription itself, use Google Play subscriptions.
  • Backups: Residual copies in encrypted system backups are purged on our provider's normal backup rotation, no later than 30 days after deletion.

Apart from the records above, no personal data is retained after your deletion request is completed.
